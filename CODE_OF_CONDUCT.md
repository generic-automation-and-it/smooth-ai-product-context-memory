# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, never the person.

This project is small and mostly asynchronous, so tone carries further than you think. A review
comment that would sound fine across a desk can read as contempt in a thread three days later.
Write for the reader you cannot see.

## Expected

- Give and take feedback gracefully. A rejected patch is not a rejected person.
- Be precise rather than dismissive — "this breaks X because Y" beats "this is wrong".
- Respect that people have different experience levels, time zones and first languages.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by a mistake, and move on.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or reviews.

## Scope

Applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, reviews, commit messages and
discussions — and whenever you are representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers of
[`generic-automation-and-it`](https://github.com/generic-automation-and-it). Reports are handled
confidentially, and you will not be asked to confront the other party.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith on a report may face consequences themselves.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may warn, temporarily
block, or permanently ban for repeated or severe behaviour. Enforcement is proportionate: a first
lapse gets a private word, not a ban.
